The learning science

Test at the edge of forgetting.

Memory decays along a curve. Recall something just before it slips and the curve flattens — each successful retrieval buys exponentially more time. That single fact is the whole design.

Retrieval, not recognition

You commit to a country on the world map before anything is revealed. Recalling beats re-reading — it's the act that writes memory.

Spacing beats cramming

A meta you hit cold returns in three days, then a week, then a month. The awkward gap is the point — effort at ~90% odds is where memory hardens.

Interleaving forces discrimination

Rounds mix countries and clue families, so context never carries you. Thai pole or Sri Lankan pole is exactly what duels will ask.

An honest graduation bar

A tier unlocks when 80% of its metas survive a week untouched. Confidence that hasn't been slept on doesn't count.
